Stay miles ahead of your lease.

Log your odometer now and then. Miles Ahead tells you whether you're under your allowance, how much is left this month, and what you'd pay if you kept driving at this pace.

A closer look

Ahead or over, at a glance

Your allowance grows every day of the lease. The app compares it with your latest reading and says how far ahead or over you are.

This month's miles

See how many of this lease month's miles you have used and how many are left, month by month back to the start.

What an overage would cost

Enter your per-mile charge and the app projects your pace to the end of the lease and shows the fee, before it happens.

Your lease, as written

Any length in months (36, 39, 42...) and any yearly mileage allowance.

More than one car

Track every leased car in the household, each with its own terms and history.

Private by design

No account, no servers, no tracking. Your readings stay on your iPhone. A monthly reminder nudges you to log.
